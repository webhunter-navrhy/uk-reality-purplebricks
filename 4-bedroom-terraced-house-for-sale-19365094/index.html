The Property

A well-proportioned four-bedroom town house situated on a popular residential development on the northern outskirts of Lincoln, offering generous living space together with a private rear garden, garage and driveway parking.

The ground floor has an entrance hall with access to a downstairs WC, a separate kitchen/breakfast room and a particularly spacious living/dining room. The main reception room provides plenty of space for both seating and dining, with French doors opening onto the rear garden.

Upstairs are four bedrooms, including a main bedroom with an en-suite, along with a family bathroom. The layout also offers useful flexibility for families, guests or those needing space to work from home.

Outside, the enclosed rear garden has a lawn and patio seating area, with access through to the single garage. The driveway in front of the garage provides additional off-road parking.

The property is well placed for local shops, schools and everyday amenities, with good access to Lincoln and the surrounding area.
